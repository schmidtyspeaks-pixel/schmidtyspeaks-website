Meet the man

Passionate, authentic, and relatable. A voice for the moments that shape us.

From his first adrenaline-filled speech in high school to leading and inspiring thousands during a 20-year leadership career at Rocket Companies, Schmidty has learned how to move a room with storytelling, heartfelt authenticity, and contagious energy.

He speaks with a raw, real-world perspective on leadership, self-development, mental resilience, and personal growth. Audiences leave emotionally moved, reflective, and ready to take action toward positive change.

Whether it's a keynote at a leadership conference, a talk at a university, or an immersive Elevate event that blends music and motivation, the goal is the same: a moment that stays with you long after the final word.

Signature keynote

Charting the Course to Our Future

A story-led keynote about calibrating your business and your life toward the best version of yourself. Schmidty weaves family roots, sailing with his father, and lessons with “Little G” into a practical framework for reflection and direction.

  • “Smooth seas never made a skilled sailor”
  • The three types of reflection: past, present, and future
  • “The next shot is your best shot”
  • “How you finish is who you are”
