A speaker who has carried the number

Sales teams can tell within a minute whether a speaker has ever done the job. Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt has. He spent 22 years at Rocket Mortgage: more than a decade as a Director and Senior Director of Banking, six years as a Regional Vice President of Sales, and then a return to the front line as a Senior Director of Banking and Triple Crown Mortgage Banker. He knows what a slow month feels like, what a great sales culture sounds like, and what leaders need to hear before a big year.

That is why his talks land at sales kickoffs and leadership meetings. The stories are real, the humor is honest, and the takeaways are things people can use the same week.

Sales kickoffs

A kickoff has one job: send people into the year believing in the plan and in themselves. Schmidty's kickoff keynote focuses on mindset and momentum. He talks about the habits that separate top producers, about “the next shot is your best shot” after a lost deal, and about why sales and entrepreneurship have so much in common. He can open the meeting to set the tone or close it to send people out with energy.

Leadership offsites and summits

For leaders, the conversation goes deeper. Schmidty speaks on leadership and culture: how to lead with heart, help others recognize their potential, and build teams that thrive through change. He brings in the three types of reflection (past, present, and future) as a practical tool leaders can use with their own teams. For offsites, the keynote pairs naturally with an Elevate to Action workshop, so leaders leave with written commitments, not just notes.

Formats for corporate events

  • Keynote speaking, 30 to 60 minutes, for opening or closing sessions.
  • Workshops and breakouts with a take-home workbook.
  • Panel moderation for leadership, customer, or partner events.
  • Elevate, the live music and motivation experience, for celebrations and awards nights.

Topics corporate audiences ask for

Most corporate requests fall into four areas: leadership and culture, overcoming adversity, motivation and mindset, and mental health and wellness. Schmidty is open about vulnerability as a strength, which makes room for honest conversations many teams do not get to have at work. As one Rocket Mortgage leader put it after a session, every banker she talked to afterward told her how much his words meant.

A leadership keynote speaker based in Ohio

Schmidty is based in Westlake, just outside Cleveland, Ohio, and serves events across the region, including Cleveland, Akron, Columbus, Toledo, Pittsburgh, and Detroit, and anywhere within a day's drive. If you are looking for a sales kickoff speaker or a leadership keynote speaker in Ohio who speaks the language of sales, start with a quick conversation about your audience.
