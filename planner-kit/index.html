Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt is a keynote speaker, workshop leader, and the founder of Schmidty Speaks, based in Westlake, just outside Cleveland, Ohio. His mission is simple: create unforgettable moments that change lives.

Schmidty has spent 22 years at Rocket Mortgage. He served as a Director and Senior Director of Banking, led as a Regional Vice President of Sales, and today works the front line as a Triple Crown Mortgage Banker. Along the way, he learned how to lead and inspire people with storytelling, heartfelt authenticity, and contagious energy.

A graduate of Michigan State University with a BS in Communication, Schmidty speaks on leadership and culture, overcoming adversity, mental health and wellness, motivation and mindset, and youth and education. His signature keynote, Charting the Course to Our Future, uses sailing stories and the three types of reflection to help audiences find direction. His Elevate to Action workshops send people home with written commitments, and Elevate, his live music and motivation experience, puts a DJ on stage beside him.

Off stage, he is a proud dad to his two kids, “The Gs,” and a lifelong student of the next adventure.

A/V & logistics

A simple checklist for your A/V team.

Every venue is different. Schmidty will confirm the details on your planning call, and he is happy to talk directly with your A/V or production team.

For every session

  • Wireless microphone. A lavalier (preferred) or a handheld, with fresh batteries and a spare on hand.
  • Room to move. A stage or open front area where Schmidty can walk and connect with the audience.
  • HDMI at the stage if slides or video are used, plus a wireless clicker if available.
  • Confidence monitor facing the stage, if available.
  • Audio from a laptop through the house sound for walk-on music or video clips.
  • Stage lighting that lets the audience see his face, with house lights up enough for interaction.
  • Water and a stool or small table on or near the stage.
  • A quick tech check before doors, with someone from A/V present.

For Elevate (live music experience)

  • DJ setup. A table, power at the stage, and a sound system or PA sized for the room.
  • Mixer inputs so the DJ and Schmidty's mic run through the same system.
  • A full sound check with the DJ before guests arrive.

Workshops

  • Tables or seating where participants can write, plus pens.
  • Printed workbooks for each participant, coordinated on your planning call.

Planning to record or livestream? Let us know ahead of time so it can be discussed and planned.
