The talk that keeps working after the room clears

A great keynote can change how people feel. A workshop changes what they do on Monday. Elevate to Action takes the stories and ideas from Schmidty's keynotes and turns them into a working session, with time to think, write, talk with peers, and commit to specific next steps.

Every participant gets a workbook, and every section of the workshop ends the same way: three takeaways and three action commitments. By the end, each person leaves with a short, personal plan written in their own words, not a stack of slides they will never open again.

How a session runs

  • Set the heading. Schmidty opens with a story and frames the challenge the group is facing, whether that is a new year, a new role, or a tough stretch.
  • Reflect. Participants work through the three types of reflection (past, present, and future) with guided prompts in the workbook.
  • Share. Small-group conversations turn private insights into shared language for the team.
  • Commit. Each section closes with three takeaways and three action commitments, captured on the page.

From the classroom to the boardroom

Elevate to Action has been run with college sales students and with corporate leadership teams, and the clips on this page come from both rooms. In the classroom, Schmidty talks about simple habits that separate top performers: just show up, find the person who does it best, and ask for the desk next to the top producer. In the boardroom, the conversation turns to legacy and resilience, including his father's influence and the idea of making it one bad day, not two.

That range comes from experience. Schmidty spent 22 years at Rocket Mortgage, including six years as a Regional Vice President of Sales and more than a decade as a Director and Senior Director of Banking. He has coached new bankers and led leaders, and he builds workshops around the conversations that actually move people.

Formats and customization

Workshops can run as a breakout during a conference, a standalone session for a leadership team, or a follow-up to a Charting the Course keynote the same day. They also work as part of a custom curriculum for schools and leadership retreats. Schmidty tailors the prompts, examples, and length to the group, and the workbook can be adapted to your theme. Popular fits include sales kickoffs and leadership offsites and college sales programs.

Try the workbook first

Want a feel for the format? The free 3 Types of Reflection mini-workbook uses the same structure as the workshop: a framing for each type of reflection, three prompts, room to write, and a page for your three action commitments. It is a useful preview for planners and a good pre-read before a session.
