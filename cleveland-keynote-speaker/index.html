A keynote speaker who lives here

Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt is based in Westlake, on Cleveland's west side. When you book him for a Cleveland event, you get a speaker who knows the city and does not need a flight or a travel day to reach your stage. That makes planning simpler, and it makes it easy to add a workshop, a walkthrough of your venue, or a pre-event call with your team.

Cleveland is also where Elevate was born. Schmidty's live music and motivation experience has filled The Brothers Lounge, with a DJ on stage and stories that hit home.

Areas served

Schmidty speaks throughout Northeast Ohio and across the region, including:

  • Cleveland
  • Akron
  • Columbus
  • Toledo
  • Pittsburgh
  • Detroit
  • Anywhere within a day's drive

For events farther away, reach out with your dates and location and he will let you know what is possible.

What Cleveland organizers book

  • Charting the Course to Our Future, the signature keynote on reflection, resilience, and direction.
  • Elevate, the live music and motivation experience, for community nights and celebrations.
  • Elevate to Action workshops with a take-home workbook for leadership and sales teams.
  • Panel moderation and other guest appearances.
  • Custom curriculum for schools and leadership retreats.

Stages close to home

Schmidty's stages include The University of Akron's Fisher Sales Society, Ohio University, The Brothers Lounge in Cleveland, and teams at Rocket Mortgage, where he has spent 22 years as a banker, sales leader, and Regional Vice President of Sales. He has also worked with Questbank in Canada and taken the stage at the TEDx Speak Off.

Audiences across Ohio

Schmidty speaks to corporate teams and leadership summits; association conferences in financial services, mortgage, banking, real estate, and HR; college sales programs and business schools; high schools, educators, coaches, and youth mentors; and community organizations. Whatever the room, the goal is the same: a moment that stays with people long after the final word.
