Elevate: live music meets motivation

An immersive night that blends live music and motivation. Part show, part keynote, with a DJ on stage and stories that hit home.

Book Schmidty Watch

Part show, part keynote

Elevate is the experience Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt built for people who have sat through enough slide decks. A DJ sets the energy, the lights come down, and Schmidty takes the stage with stories about family, failure, leadership, and finding your way back. The music is not background. It moves the room between moments, gives people space to breathe, and helps the message land.

Elevate started in Cleveland at The Brothers Lounge, which has hosted several editions, including Elevate 2025 and Elevate Part 2: Lessons with Little G. The videos and clips on this page come straight from those nights.

What happens in the room

Each Elevate is built around a theme, then shaped like a set list. A night typically moves through a few chapters:

  • The open. The DJ builds the energy before Schmidty says a word, so the room arrives ready.
  • The stories. Real, unpolished stories about growing up, sailing with his dad, raising “The Gs,” and two decades of leading teams at Rocket Mortgage.
  • The pause. Moments designed to slow down, breathe, and reflect, like the “Elevate and breathe” segment you can watch below.
  • The challenge. A clear call to action, so people leave with something to do, not just something they felt.

Why music and motivation work together

Music changes how people listen. It lowers the guard most audiences bring to a talk, and it helps them remember where they were when a line hit home. Schmidty leans into vulnerability on stage (“Vulnerability isn't weakness. It's the strongest thing you can do on purpose.”) and the music gives the room permission to feel it with him.

Bring Elevate to your event

Elevate works as a standalone community night, a closing session for a conference, a team celebration, or a kickoff that needs to feel different from last year. It can run in a lounge, a ballroom, or an auditorium. The core ingredients are a stage, a sound system, and a DJ setup, and Schmidty will talk through the right plan with your team. The Planner Kit includes an A/V checklist that covers what Elevate needs.

If your group wants the energy of Elevate with more structure for follow-through, pair it with an Elevate to Action workshop and the free reflection workbook.

Topics that show up on stage

Every Elevate is different, but the threads stay the same: leadership and culture, overcoming adversity, mental health and wellness, and motivation and mindset. The goal is a night people talk about the next day, and a message they still use the next month. Planning something in Northeast Ohio? See Cleveland events.
