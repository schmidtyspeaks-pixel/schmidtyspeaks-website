Colleges & universities

College speaker for sales programs and student leaders

Real talk about sales, leadership, and growth for business schools, sales programs, and student organizations, from a speaker who spent two decades coaching and leading sales teams.

Book Schmidty Watch

The talk of the town the next day

When Schmidty spoke to the Fisher Sales Society at The University of Akron, the program's VP wrote the next morning that his presentation was “EPIC” and that he was “the talk of the town today around the Fisher Institute.” That reaction is what he aims for with every student audience: stories that hit home, and take-home messages students actually use.

He has also spoken at Ohio University, where students heard his message on perspective, and with students connected to The Schey Sales Centre, where one strategic leadership major called the motivation he sparked “something I'll never forget.”

Why students listen

Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt earned a BS in Communication from Michigan State University, then spent 22 years at Rocket Mortgage. He became a Director and Senior Director of Banking, served as a Regional Vice President of Sales, and returned to the front line as a Triple Crown Mortgage Banker. Students hear from someone who has been the new hire, the producer, and the leader making hiring decisions.

He does not lecture. He tells stories, makes people laugh, admits what he got wrong, and gives straight answers about what it takes to succeed in a first job.

Topics for college audiences

  • Sales as a career. Why sales and entrepreneurship have so much in common, and how sales skills open doors in any field.
  • Habits of top performers. Just show up. Find the person who does it best. Ask for the desk next to the top producer.
  • Leadership and potential. How to lead teams and help others recognize their potential, even before you have a title.
  • Resilience and reflection. The three types of reflection (past, present, and future) as a tool for choosing a path.
  • Nerves and confidence. Everyone gets nervous, including speakers. Here is how to use it.

Formats for campuses

Schmidty speaks at sales society meetings, business school classes, student leadership conferences, career-readiness programs, and campus-wide events. Sessions can be a 30 to 60 minute keynote, an interactive Elevate to Action workshop with a take-home workbook, a panel, or a custom curriculum across several sessions. Faculty and student organizers can use the free 3 Types of Reflection workbook as a pre-read or a follow-up activity.

For faculty and program directors

Sales and leadership programs work hard to connect classroom learning to real careers. Schmidty is that bridge. He can tailor a session to your curriculum, join a class discussion after a keynote, or speak at a networking night with employers in the room. Ask about pairing a campus keynote with a session for your student leaders.

Serving Ohio and the region

Based in Westlake near Cleveland, Schmidty serves colleges and universities throughout Ohio and the region. Planning for high school students too? He speaks to younger audiences as well. Use the booking form to share your program, audience size, and dates.
