Keynotes that speak your members' language

Association audiences are smart, busy, and quick to spot a generic speech. They want a speaker who understands their industry and still gives them something bigger to think about. Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt brings both. He spent 22 years at Rocket Mortgage as a banker, sales leader, and Regional Vice President of Sales, and he turns that experience into keynotes about leadership, resilience, and growth.

Financial services, mortgage, banking, and real estate

For mortgage, banking, lending, and real estate conferences, Schmidty is an insider. He has worked through changing rate environments, shifting markets, and the pressure of production goals, and he has led bankers through busy seasons and lean ones. His message for these audiences centers on what keeps professionals steady when the market is not: purpose, reflection, and the habits that build long careers.

He also speaks to the human side of the business. Mortgage and real estate professionals help people with some of the biggest decisions of their lives, and they often carry that stress home. Schmidty's talks make room for mental health and wellness, and for the idea that vulnerability is a strength.

HR, people leaders, and L&D audiences

For HR and learning and development conferences, Schmidty focuses on leadership and culture: how to lead with heart, help people recognize their potential, and build workplaces where people want to stay. His Elevate to Action workshop format, with a take-home workbook and three action commitments per section, gives L&D professionals a model they can bring back to their own organizations.

Opening, closing, and breakout sessions

  • Opening keynote. Charting the Course to Our Future sets the tone, using sailing stories and the three types of reflection to frame the conference.
  • Closing keynote. “How you finish is who you are” sends attendees home with energy and a plan.
  • Breakouts and workshops. Interactive sessions that turn ideas into action commitments.
  • Panel moderation. Conversational, honest panels that stay on time.
  • Evening events. Elevate, the live music and motivation experience, makes for a memorable conference night.

Easy to work with

Conference planners juggle a lot of speakers. Schmidty makes his part simple: a planning call to learn about your members and your theme, materials delivered when you need them, and a message tailored to the moment your industry is in. The Planner Kit has short, medium, and long bios, photos, an introduction script, a one-sheet, and an A/V checklist ready for your program.

A conference keynote speaker based in Ohio

Schmidty is based in Westlake, near Cleveland, and serves conferences across Ohio and the region, including Cleveland, Columbus, Akron, Toledo, Pittsburgh, and Detroit, and anywhere within a day's drive. If you are searching for a conference keynote speaker in Ohio for a state or regional association meeting, share your dates and audience through the booking form.
