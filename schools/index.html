It started with a speech in high school

Schmidty's speaking story starts in a high school, with his first adrenaline-filled speech. He still remembers the nerves, and he talks about them openly with students, because everyone gets nervous. That honesty is the point. Young people can tell when an adult is performing for them. They lean in when someone tells the truth.

Today, Geoff “Schmidty” Schmidt is a dad of two, a former Regional Vice President of Sales, and a speaker who spent 22 years leading and developing people at Rocket Mortgage. He brings all of that back to the classroom, as he did on the “Back to high school” visit you can watch on this page.

What students take away

  • Find your voice and your value. Confidence is built, not born, and nerves are a sign you care.
  • “Smooth seas never made a skilled sailor.” Hard seasons build the skills you will need later.
  • Just show up. Small, consistent actions open more doors than talent alone.
  • “The next shot is your best shot.” A bad test, game, or day does not define you. Make it one bad day, not two.
  • Reflection. Looking at the past, present, and future to choose what comes next.

For educators, coaches, and youth mentors

The adults who show up for young people every day need support too. Schmidty speaks to educators, coaches, and youth mentors about leading with heart, building cultures where students thrive, and taking care of their own mental health and wellness. These sessions fit staff development days, coaching clinics, and back-to-school kickoffs.

Formats for schools and youth programs

Schmidty offers assemblies and keynotes of 30 to 60 minutes, smaller classroom or leadership-group sessions, and custom curriculum for schools and leadership retreats. Workshops can use the Elevate to Action workbook format, where every section ends with three takeaways and three action commitments, and the reflection prompts can be adapted to school, sports, and friendships.

Youth programs, athletic teams, student councils, and community organizations are all a good fit. If you are planning an event for students and want a speaker who is relatable, funny, and real, start with the booking form.

Mental health, handled with care

Schmidty talks about mental health and wellness with care and without lecturing. He covers vulnerability, asking for help, and showing up for others. Before every school event, he connects with organizers to understand the audience and any topics that need extra sensitivity.

Before and after the assembly

The best school visits do not end when the bell rings. Teachers and counselors can use the free 3 Types of Reflection workbook as a classroom follow-up, and older students heading to college can learn more on the college speaking page. Schmidty is based near Cleveland and serves schools across Ohio and the region.
